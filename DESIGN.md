# Design

## Audience

Early-career developers who are worried about AI. They are juniors or recent graduates who feel uncertain about layoffs, and who wonder whether their skills still matter now that AI can write and build software.

Where they watch: mostly on a phone, in vertical (9:16) format, on social platforms. The horizontal (16:9) cut is for larger screens.

Language: the narration is in Amharic, with English subtitles.

## Intent

When the film ends, a viewer should feel hopeful: they can still build with AI. The story starts with doubt and ends with a turn toward learning to build with it.

The viewer should understand this in one sentence: AI changed how software is built, and the engineers who learn to build with it still have a place.

Every shot should serve that feeling.

## The story

A first-person story, told by one developer:

1. In 2022 AI arrives, and the narrator thinks it can never match a developer.
2. The narrator dismisses it and doesn't think it will get this powerful.
3. Companies start laying people off, and AI models keep getting stronger.
4. The narrator questions their own ability to do the work.
5. Building with AI turns from writing code into prompting, and their skills slip away.
6. The turn: the narrator stops watching and starts experimenting. Maybe the future is learning to build with AI. The world is changing, and so can we.

## What the film shows, and what it doesn't

- The film is a story. It does not show the pipeline on screen, except for the end card.
- The end card says the film was made with AI.
- The public post and the repo carry the pipeline. The post links to the repo.
- The on-screen developer is an invented character with no real face. No real person appears or is voiced without a recorded consent.

## Length and format

- Length: 60 to 90 seconds. Narration is measured against this with the Gemini TTS output.
- Shots: 12, with one idea per shot.
- Formats: 9:16 vertical as the main cut, and 16:9 horizontal.

## Decisions made so far

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Early-career developers worried about AI | The story speaks directly to them |
| Intent | Hopeful: they can still build with AI | The draft ends on learning to build with it |
| Pipeline on screen | Only the end card, and the post | Keeps the film a story; the repo carries the craft |
| Narration language | Amharic, English subtitles | Your choice; the story is yours |
| Characters | One invented developer, no real face | No consent record needed |
| Shots | 12 | Your choice, fits 60–90 seconds |
