    Local studio · free-tier models

    Make a short film from a story

    Write a few sentences, or paste a script you already have. The studio drafts an 8–12 shot storyboard, records Amharic narration (or English), illustrates every shot, animates the keyframes, scores the film, burns in English subtitles and renders both vertical and horizontal cuts.

    Everything runs locally on free-tier models. A full film takes a few minutes, and every shot stays editable afterwards.
